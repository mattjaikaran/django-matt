   - In `user_joined()`, after updating forward index, also update reverse index:
     ```python
     reverse_key = f"{self._prefix}user:{user_id}:groups"

     cache.set(reverse_key, user_groups, timeout=86400)
     ```
   - In `user_left()`, after updating forward index, also update reverse index:
     ```python
     reverse_key = f"{self._prefix}user:{user_id}:groups"
     user_groups = cache.get(reverse_key) or set()

     if user_groups:
         cache.set(reverse_key, user_groups, timeout=86400)
     else:
         cache.delete(reverse_key)
     ```
   - Replace `get_user_groups()` body to read from reverse index:
     ```python
     cache = self._get_cache()
     ```
